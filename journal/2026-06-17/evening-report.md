## Lesson of the day

FOMC day validated the principle behind the VST structural stop. The stop was set at $137 — the structural base-failure level, not an arbitrary event-avoidance buffer. The distinction mattered: a stop set to "avoid the FOMC whipsaw" would have needed to be either very tight (getting stopped out on normal volatility) or set below any realistic FOMC move (making it too wide to be meaningful). Instead, the stop was sized to the thesis: if FOMC or any other event breaks the 4-week base, the thesis is dead regardless of cause. The event came and went; the thesis held. One clean data point that a fundamentals-based structural stop can survive a binary macro event without either getting stopped out prematurely or losing its meaning.

The MRVL pass also reinforced the firm's extended-entry doctrine: a name up 75% in a month on light volume, flagged by the scanner on a +5.77% day, moved lower on the close. The scanner finds the move; the committee provides the discipline.
