CATALYST: Sanofi halts MOBILIZE Phase 3 trial of riliprubart (SAR445088), a competing C1s inhibitor for CIDP refractory to IVIg, due to insufficient efficacy from interim DMC review

TYPE: sector-sympathy (negative catalyst for rival asset threatens DNTH's comparable lead program claseprubart)

CONFIDENCE: high

SUMMARY: On June 10, 2026, Sanofi announced termination of the MOBILIZE Phase 3 trial of riliprubart in CIDP-refractory patients following an interim data monitoring committee review that found the trial unlikely to deliver sufficient efficacy. Riliprubart is a structurally similar C1s complement inhibitor to Dianthus's lead asset claseprubart (both monoclonal antibodies targeting the classical complement pathway for the same CIDP indication). The announcement triggered a 16.4% sell-off on 7.4x volume as investors repriced efficacy and competitive risk for claseprubart in the same indication. No safety signals were identified for riliprubart.

READ-THROUGH: The selloff appears moderately overreacted. Riliprubart's failure does not directly validate claseprubart's efficacy—both are early in their CIDP journeys and Dianthus reports its own CAPTIVATE study made an early GO decision in Part A (positive signal). However, the failure of a structurally similar drug in the same indication creates legitimate efficacy uncertainty in the pathway and validates concerns about whether C1s inhibition is sufficient in CIDP. Stifel reiterated a Buy rating on DNTH post-announcement, suggesting sell-side sees the move as overdone.
