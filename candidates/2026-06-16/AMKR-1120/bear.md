# AMKR — Bear Review (Pass 1)
_Risk manager | 2026-06-16_

---

OBJECTIONS:

1. **Lesson #8 violation — volume signature is the inverse of the positive-expectancy template.**
   The backtested momentum-continuation rule requires a 2.5× volume surge at the initial breakout.
   The initial catalyst day (Jun 9) printed only 1.58× volume. Today — the largest single-day
   percentage gain in the 8-day run (+11.0%) — printed 1.16× volume, the LOWEST multiple of any
   up-day in the run. The technicals memo documents this explicitly: "biggest up-days came on
   1.00×, 1.39×, and 1.16× volume respectively." Volume is DECLINING as price advances. Lesson #8
   says momentum continuation has a measured positive edge; it also says the firm now knows the
   conditions under which that edge exists. This setup does not meet those conditions. Entering
   here is buying a pattern that fails the firm's own backtest filter.

2. **Entry geometry is broken — R:R sub-1:1, a HARD objection at any tier.**
   The technical analyst's own numbers: stop at $86.18 (today's gap-open shelf, the only logical
   exit if the move fades), $5.74 of risk per share; nearest definable resistance at $96.66
   (HOD / 52w high), $4.74 of reward. R:R = 0.83:1. This is below 1:1 and materially below
   the 1.2:1 minimum that constitutes broken arithmetic under the exploratory-tier rules.
   The analyst acknowledges this: "R:R at entry ~4.74/5.74 = 0.83:1 — less than 1:1 on a
   first-target basis." A second target requires projecting into open air with no overhead
   structure — that is not a target, it is a hope. Broken arithmetic is a hard objection
   regardless of tier.

3. **FOMC decision tomorrow (June 17, Warsh's first) — standing event risk.**
   FOMC decisions create gap risk for any overnight position. The news analyst flags this
   explicitly: "the risk of a 'buy the news, sell the fact' consolidation or a post-FOMC flush
   is real." A hawkish surprise or even an ambiguous statement from a new Fed chair who has no
   established market read could gap semiconductor/risk assets down 2–4% at the open. The stop
   at $86.18 assumes an orderly exit; a gap through that level fills at market (no limit-stop
   protection on gaps), and the actual loss on the position could be materially worse than the
   stated 6.2% risk. The firm cannot size out of gap risk — it is a binary, uncontrollable
   event that arrives overnight. Juneteenth on Friday (June 19) compresses the exit window
   further: if the FOMC reaction is adverse on Wednesday, the next full session is not until
   Monday, extending gap exposure.

4. **Load-bearing source re-fetch: deal scope is vaguer than the memos imply — no financial
   terms, no volume commitments, no exclusivity.**
   The news memo's EVIDENCE block quotes the deal as covering "TSMC to procure advanced
   packaging and testing services from Amkor" and describes it as a "highly stable and premium
   customer base" that "mitigates long-term execution and underutilization risks." The StockTitan
   re-fetch of the primary press release confirms the 10-year term and the services scope, but
   also confirms: zero dollar amounts, zero revenue commitments, zero volume commitments, zero
   pricing terms, and zero exclusivity language. The release says only that the collaboration
   should create "a more efficient, mutually beneficial operating model." The memos characterize
   this as TSMC becoming a committed anchor customer for the Arizona facility; the press release
   says only that TSMC will procure from Amkor — it does not say how much, at what price, or
   whether Amkor is the sole source. This is a framework agreement, not a supply contract.
   Framing it as risk-mitigation for underutilization is the analyst's inference, not the
   document's language. The catalyst is real; its magnitude is overstated.

5. **Stock is 9% above the mean analyst price target on day 8 of a 41% run — the market is
   pricing in execution that has not occurred.**
   The news memo quotes: "The mean analyst price of $75.50 now sitting roughly 9% below where
   the stock trades. The market is ahead of the analysts." This is not a bull argument — it is
   a warning that every Street model already baking in known information comes out $75.50, not
   $92. Buying 9% above consensus fair value on day 8 of a 41% run means the firm is paying
   for the dream version of a framework agreement that disclosed no financial terms. The gap
   between price and target will eventually close — either via target upgrades (which will
   require actual revenue from the TSMC deal materializing, likely 2027+) or via price
   reversion. Entering here is volunteering to be the marginal buyer at the top of a premium.

6. **Intraday gap-and-fade is an exhaustion signal, not a breakout signal.**
   The stock opened at $86.21, ran to $96.66 (HOD / 52w high), then retreated to close near
   $91.92 — a 5.2% fade from the high on the LOWEST relative volume of the run. The technical
   analyst correctly reads this as "consistent with an exhaustion/distribution pattern rather
   than a clean breakout that holds." A real breakout-continuation closes near HOD. This one
   reversed 5.2% from the high on average volume. We would be entering at a price that has
   already been rejected intraday.

7. **Sentiment data is Stocktwits-only — Lesson #2 applies, mildly.**
   The sentiment analyst notes Reddit API was unavailable and flags: "Reddit-silent name with
   only Stocktwits chatter carries higher narrative-fabrication risk." Lesson #2 states a dark
   data channel must be reported dark, not paraphrased from other sources. The analyst correctly
   reports the gap rather than fabricating a read, so this is not a fabrication objection. It
   is, however, a data quality flag: the contrarian read is built on 30 posts from a single
   platform. The crowd thesis (bullish consensus) cannot be confirmed or denied with confidence.
   This objection alone would not block the trade; it weakens the sentiment pillar.

8. **watchlist: false — no declared edge on this name.**
   AMKR is not on Diego's thesis universe. The firm has no pre-existing informational or
   analytical edge here. The committee found it because the scanner flagged a move that had
   already run 41% over 8 days. The committee is chasing a name it knew nothing about 8 days
   ago, at day 8, on a framework deal with no disclosed financial terms, into an FOMC night.
   This is the definition of lesson #4: "interesting but unconvincing" should be a PASS, not
   an entry.

---

SEVERITY: **Objection 2 (sub-1:1 R:R) is the single most decisive objection.**

Under the exploratory-tier rules, fabricated/unverified evidence, broken arithmetic, limits.yaml
violations, and event risk are HARD objections that survive the exploratory waiver. The technical
analyst computed R:R = 0.83:1 using the firm's own stop logic. This is broken arithmetic — the
reward does not cover the stated risk even before accounting for the gap-through risk on an FOMC
night (Objection 3), which would widen the actual loss beyond the modeled stop. The sub-1:1
geometry and FOMC gap risk together make this a structurally losing bet in expected-value terms
regardless of how compelling the underlying business story is.

Objections 1 (Lesson #8 volume failure) and 3 (FOMC gap risk) are independently material and
would each warrant a SUSTAIN on their own merits at any tier.

---

WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND:

- **Objection 1:** A re-entry after a 2–3 day consolidation that holds the $86.18 shelf with
  volume compressing, followed by a breakout above $96.66 on 2.5×+ volume — that would be
  the Lesson #8 template, not this.

- **Objection 2:** A stop tight enough to produce R:R ≥ 1.2:1, or a credible second target
  backed by measured-move projection (e.g., the breakout range width ~17 pts added above $96.66
  gives ~$113.66 as a target — but that requires assuming the breakout holds, which the
  intraday fade contradicts). The head trader would need to show the arithmetic, not assert it.

- **Objection 3:** Waiting for the FOMC decision to pass (June 17 afternoon) and re-evaluating
  on June 18. If the market absorbs FOMC well and AMKR holds above $86.18, the overnight risk
  is gone and the trade can be reassessed on cleaner geometry.

- **Objection 4:** An 8-K or supplemental filing from Amkor disclosing minimum purchase
  commitments, dollar volume, or exclusivity terms would upgrade the catalyst from "framework
  agreement" to "binding revenue commitment."

- **Objections 5 & 6:** A 2–3 session consolidation sideways above $86 that absorbs the
  extended premium and re-sets a tighter entry with better R:R.

---

VERDICT: **VETOED**

Two hard objections that survive the exploratory-tier waiver:

- **Objection 2 (broken arithmetic):** R:R = 0.83:1 is below the 1.2:1 floor. This is not a
  judgment call — the technical analyst computed it using the firm's own stop logic.

- **Objection 3 (event risk):** FOMC decision June 17 (overnight from today's entry) with gap
  exposure amplified by a compressed exit window (Juneteenth Friday). Gap-through risk means
  the actual loss could exceed the modeled stop materially, making the already-broken R:R
  even worse in the tail scenario.

The catalyst is real. The business story is legitimate. The entry today is wrong.
